About:

I am a third-year computer science student at the University of Mary Washington (UMW) in Fredericksburg, Virginia. During my time at UMW I have worked on lots of exciting projects, which are accessible above.


My recent work focuses on BLVDIFF, a Rust-based command-line tool designed to enhance Python debugging for blind and low-vision (BLV) developers. It integrates an LLM for trace back explanations and differential tools. Aiming to address common debugging pain points for BLV users.
